# Visual asset credits

SealedVoice brand assets are being created for Team Fprinciples, RMIT SGS, for the 2026 RMIT Hackathon. The original seal/keyhole geometry in `public/brand/logo-mark.svg` and `app/icon.svg` was drawn directly as SVG for this project; it was not traced from an existing logo or downloaded icon.

The wax red, navy, ink and paper colours come from the project's `app/sealedvoice.css`. Font attributions, the asset inventory and export instructions will be completed with the remaining round-two artwork. No third-party icon pack or stock image is used.
